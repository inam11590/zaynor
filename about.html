The Genesis

Redefining Male Grooming

Founded in 2026, ZAYNOR was born out of a simple observation: Pakistani men deserved grooming products tailored to their unique skin, climate, and cultural heritage, without having to pay exorbitant import duties for luxury global brands.

We set out to create a premium brand that bridges the gap. By marrying the timeless, historic art of Eastern attar extraction with cutting-edge modern science, we deliver high-performance skin solutions and high-concentration fragrances that outlast the longest of days.

Our Commitments

The ZAYNOR Standards

🔮

Pure Oil Extractions

Our attars are formulated alcohol-free, using time-tested copper-pot distillation methods to yield highly concentrated, pure essences that adapt uniquely to your skin chemistry.

🛁

Toxin-Free Skincare

We strictly avoid sulfates, parabens, phthalates, and synthetic fillers. ZAYNOR skin solutions use natural botanical bases, activated charcoal, and pure vitamins.

🇵🇰

Crafted in Pakistan

From sourcing minerals from the Himalayan foothills to hand-pouring our luxury fragrance bottles in Lahore, we are proud to be a 100% Pakistani home-grown brand.

The Craftsmanship

Formulated for the Subcontinent

Traditional imported skincare is designed for cold, dry climates. In contrast, the Pakistani climate demands formulas that handle intense humidity, dry winter winds, dust, and harsh sunlight.

Every ZAYNOR formula undergoes rigorous testing on local skin types. Our moisturizers are engineered to be lightweight, fast-absorbing, and completely non-greasy, while our cleansers provide heavy-duty detoxifying action without dehydrating the epidermis.
